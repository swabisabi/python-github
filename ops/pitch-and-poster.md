# Pitch and poster (Pitch lead)

## Poster
- QR to the hub URL: `python tools/make_qr.py <hub-url> poster-qr.svg`
- Line (English): New in town? Find your team.
- Line (Swahili): Mgeni mjini? Tafuta timu yako. (have a Swahili speaker check this)
- Community invite link printed beneath the QR.

## 3-minute pitch
1. The newcomer's problem: you move, you know nobody, teams exist but you can't see them.
2. The live groups: show a town group with pinned, verified listings.
3. One real athlete's story: joined, got a reply, went to a run.
4. Reach: schools, clubs, federations and social media, all through one link.

## Demo rehearsal (done when it runs twice in a row on mobile data)
1. Scan the QR on a phone.
2. Join a town group and post an intro.
3. Show the connector's reply.
4. Show a pinned listing with its verified date.
