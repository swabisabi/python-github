# Lacy GTA 6: top 3 viral clips (shot list and caption timings)

Format: vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 30-45s each. Timings are targets; adjust to the real footage.
These are templates. I have not seen the footage, so replace the bracketed notes with what actually happens.

## Caption style (all three)
- **Hook:** huge centered text, fades IN 0.3s, holds, fades OUT 0.2s. One word in yellow or red.
- **Running captions:** 2-4 words, fade in 0.15s, fade out 0.15s, lower third (y ~ 1500px), clear of the action.
- **Blackout beat:** captions disappear 1.0s before the key moment. No text, game audio only.
- **Payoff card:** fades in 0.2s after the result, holds 1.5s.
- **Font:** bold sans-serif, black outline, white text. Max 3 colors.

## Recording and editing checklist
1. Record 1080p, 60 fps, with the HUD hidden except speed and wanted level where they help the story.
2. Capture 5-10 takes of each moment so you can pick the cleanest, and keep the failed attempts for Clip 1.
3. Cut on action. Keep every clip under 45s.
4. Add captions in CapCut or Kdenlive, or upload to OpusClip and edit its auto-captions (set fade in/out and font there).
5. Pick trending audio only if it doesn't mask the game sound during the blackout beat.
6. Check Rockstar's content policy before monetizing.
