# Script 2: Mistake teardown (bad prompts)

- **Hook type:** Mistake teardown
- **Length:** about 30 seconds

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Text: **"Your prompt is the problem"**. A vague prompt on screen, like "summarize this." | "Here's the prompt most people write, and why you get a bad answer." |
| 3-8s | Show the weak output. Circle what's wrong. | "Generic. Too long. Misses the point." |
| 8-16s | Rewrite on screen, adding three things one at a time: who it's for, what format, what to leave out. | "Fix it with three things. Say who it's for. Say the format. Say what to skip." |
| 16-23s | Run the new prompt and show the output. | "Same tool. Same text. Completely different answer." |
| 23-27s | Side by side, old versus new. | "The difference is the instructions, not the tool." |
| 27-30s | Text: **"Comment 'prompt' and I'll show another"** | "Comment 'prompt' and I'll break down another one." |

**Caption:** Same tool, same text. Different prompt. #aitips #promptengineering #chatgpt
