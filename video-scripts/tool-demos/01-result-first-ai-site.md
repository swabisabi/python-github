# Script 1: Result first (AI price card)

- **Hook type:** Result first
- **Length:** about 30 seconds
- **Subject:** the A5 "Get Your Shop Found Online" price card (`price-card/price-card.html`)
- **Note:** Replace every [X] with a real, recorded figure before posting. The existing card was built in an earlier session, so recreate it live from a fresh prompt for the recording. Do not claim the old build time.

## Recording prep

1. Move or rename `price-card/` so the old card is not on screen, or work in a new empty folder.
2. Start a screen recorder with a visible clock or timer. Record at phone-friendly 9:16 if possible.
3. Paste the prompt below, press enter, and let the recording run until the card renders.
4. Open the HTML in a browser, show it full screen, then print-preview to show the A5 layout.
5. Write down the real elapsed time and use it for [X].

## The prompt (one message)

> Make a single printable A5 portrait HTML price card for a one-person service called "Get Your Shop Found Online" for small shops in Eldoret, Kenya. Header with title and a tagline listing Google Maps, WhatsApp Business, done for you in one day. Two price tables. Google Maps Listing: Basic KES 2,000 (listing, location pin, phone, hours, category, verification), Full KES 4,000 (Basic plus photos, services, description, landmark directions). WhatsApp Business Setup: Basic KES 1,000, Standard KES 2,500 (catalogue up to 20 products, quick replies, labels), Premium KES 3,500 (photos, catalogue link, QR code, 30-minute training). A highlighted bundle box: Maps Basic plus WhatsApp Standard for KES 3,800, saving KES 700. Three short "why it pays for itself" bullets, a terms line (50% by M-Pesa to start, 50% when live, customer owns the listing, no reading of customer chats), and blank Name and WhatsApp lines at the bottom. Green and blue colours, clean and readable, prints on one page with no margins.

This is the same content as the existing card, so you can compare the fresh result with the old one.
